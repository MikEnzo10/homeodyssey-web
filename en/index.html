How it works

A week with HomeOdyssey

Set it up in five minutes on Monday. For the rest of the week, the app keeps score.

1

Plan the week's adventure

Create your home, add your kids and pick their missions. No need to come up with them: there are ready-made ideas and packs for every age.

  • Daily habits and special missions
  • You decide how many stars each one is worth
  • It can repeat itself every week
2

Kids complete their missions

Each kid sees what's on today and ticks it off when it's done. On your phone (in kid mode, with their PIN) or on their own.

  • Make the bed, set the table, read for a while…
  • Team missions for brothers and sisters
  • Notifications when there's something new
3

You approve it in seconds

Stars arrive when an adult approves. No photo proof: you trust them and check it with one tap.

  • Approve them all at once, or one by one
  • Another adult at home can do it too
  • If something isn't done, send it back
4

At the end of the week, a big celebration

When the week closes, stars are shared out and the podium goes to whoever completed the biggest share of their missions. So a 6-year-old and a 12-year-old compete fairly.

  • A podium for effort, not quantity
  • A bonus for completing everything
  • Then the next adventure begins
What keeps them hooked

They save up for something big and level up

The star piggy bank

They spend on small rewards or save for a big prize: a bike, a Lego set, a day at a theme park. They learn to wait for what they want.

Levels, streaks and badges

Every mission brings them closer to the next level. Their avatar, title and badges grow with them, and the streak rewards keeping it up.

On a phone or on paper

Your kids don't need to use screens if you'd rather they didn't

Print their weekly sheet, stick it on the fridge and let them tick it with a pen. Then copy it into the app in a minute and the stars arrive just the same.

📱

On your phone or a tablet

They open their profile, with a PIN if you like, and tick off what they've done. You switch back to grown-up mode with your fingerprint, Face ID or password.

📲

On their own phone

Connect it once with a code. They don't need an email or an account.

🖨️

On paper

One sheet per kid, with their morning, afternoon and evening missions. Included in the free plan too.

FAQ

Questions parents ask us

What ages is it for?

It's designed for kids aged 6 to 14. Mission and reward ideas are filtered by age, and you can always create your own for younger or older kids.

Do my kids need a phone?

No. They can use your phone: switch it to kid mode, and switching back to grown-up mode needs your fingerprint, your phone PIN or your password. If they have their own phone or tablet, they can sign in there with their profile too.

How do I know they've really done it?

Your kid ticks off the mission and an adult approves it. Stars only arrive once you approve. If something isn't done, send it back and they try again.

Can my partner or the grandparents use it too?

Yes. Invite other adults to your home and everyone can see the missions, approve them and give rewards. Each adult signs in with their own account.

What happens when the free launch ends?

We'll let you know in the app well in advance. Then you choose: stay on the free plan forever or move to Plus, with a 14-day trial before you pay. We never charge you unless you choose to.

Is it on iPhone?

It's out on Android now and the iPhone version is coming very soon. Follow us on Instagram or TikTok to hear about it on day one.

Which languages is it in?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French and German, including the mission ideas and notifications.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile › Delete my account. Your account, your home and your kids' profiles are deleted straight away. There are more details on how to delete your account.
