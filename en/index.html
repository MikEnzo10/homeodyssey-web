How it works

Household chores, stars and a reward at the end

Set it up in five minutes on Monday. For the rest of the week, the app keeps score.

1

Plan the week's adventure

Create your home, add your kids and pick their missions and what they can earn. No need to come up with anything: there are ready-made ideas and packs for every age.

  • Daily habits, routines and special missions
  • You decide how many stars each one is worth
  • The balance suggests a fair price for every reward
2

Kids complete their missions

Each kid sees what's on today and ticks it off when it's done: make the bed, set the table, tidy up the toys… On your phone, on their own, or on paper.

  • Special missions worth more stars
  • Team missions for brothers and sisters
  • You get a notification when they finish
3

You approve it and the stars arrive

No photo proof: you trust them, check it with one tap and the stars land in their account. If something isn't done, send it back.

  • Approve them all at once, or one by one
  • Your partner, and even the grandparents, can too
  • Bonus stars if they complete the whole week
4

And they swap them for their reward

Small ones, like picking Saturday's film, or big ones: they save in their piggy bank for the bike or the day at the theme park. They learn to choose and to wait for what they want.

  • Rewards you choose, and many cost nothing
  • The piggy bank, to save for the big stuff
  • They suggest, you decide
On Sunday

At the end of the week, a big celebration

Stars are shared out and the podium goes to whoever completed the biggest share of their missions, not whoever did the most. So a 6-year-old and a 12-year-old compete fairly. Then the next adventure begins.

On a phone or on paper

Your kids don't need to use screens if you'd rather they didn't

Every home has its own rules about screens, and HomeOdyssey fits yours. You plan the week in the app, and they can live it on the fridge door.

  1. 🖨️

    Their sheet, ready to print

    One page per kid, with their morning, afternoon and evening missions, the stars each one is worth and the family prize.

  2. ✏️

    They tick it with a pen

    No passwords, no chargers: they do the mission and tick their box. It's their sheet, and they see it every time they open the fridge.

  3. ⭐

    You copy it into the app in a minute

    Tick what they've done, and their stars, streaks and badges count just as if they'd done it on a phone.

Rather use a phone or the family tablet? That works too: they open their profile, with a PIN if you like, and you switch back to grown-up mode with your fingerprint or Face ID. If they have their own phone, connect it once with a code. The sheet is included in the free plan.

New · Grandparents on the adventure

Grandparents can join in too, and it couldn't be easier for them

On the days they look after the kids, the missions don't stop. For grandparents, the babysitter or aunts and uncles there's one single screen: what each kid has to do today and one big button, “Done it”. No passwords to remember.

  1. 📲

    Send them the invitation over WhatsApp

    They install the app, tap “I look after the kids”, type the code and what the kids call them. That's it.

  2. ✅

    They tick it, you confirm it

    It lands in your “to review” list with their name and you approve it with one tap: stars, as always. They can't touch missions, rewards or stars.

  3. 🎮

    “Hand over the reins” for a few days

    Weekend at the grandparents'? For up to 7 days in a row, whatever they tick counts straight away, and you get a summary every evening. When it's over, the reins come back to you on their own.

FAQ

Questions parents ask us

What ages is it for?

It's designed for kids aged 5 to 15. Mission and reward ideas are filtered by age, and you can always create your own for younger or older kids.

Do my kids need a phone?

No. They can use your phone: switch it to kid mode, and switching back to grown-up mode needs your fingerprint, your phone PIN or your password. If they have their own phone or tablet, they can sign in there with their profile too.

How do I know they've really done it?

Your kid ticks off the mission and an adult approves it. Stars only arrive once you approve. If something isn't done, send it back and they try again.

Can my partner or the grandparents use it too?

Yes. Invite other adults to your home and everyone can see the missions, approve them and give rewards. Each adult signs in with their own account. For grandparents, the babysitter or aunts and uncles there's “Grandparents on the adventure” (Plus): a very simple screen where they just tick what the kids have done and you confirm it. They join with a link, no password needed.

Aren't the challenges just more screen time?

It's about 6 minutes a week: three one-minute challenges and a little time in their corner. They're optional: only kids play, from their own profile, and you decide whether to switch them on or off for the whole home or for each kid. Household missions are still the main thing; the challenges only add extra stars. They're part of Plus.

What rewards should I set? Do they have to cost money?

No. Many of the ideas that come with the app cost nothing: choosing dinner, Saturday's film, an extra bedtime story, an afternoon at the park… You choose which ones are in, and the balance helps you give them a fair price in stars.

What happens on January 7?

That's when the free launch ends. If you already use HomeOdyssey, you keep everything for 14 more days, until January 21, and we'll remind you in the app first. Then you choose: stay on the free plan forever or move to Plus. We never charge you unless you choose to.

Which phones does it work on?

Android, from Google Play, and iPhone and iPad, from the App Store. Each adult signs in with their own account from their own phone, whatever the brand, and everyone sees the same home.

Which languages is it in?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French and German, including the mission ideas and notifications.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile › Delete my account. Your account, your home and your kids' profiles are deleted straight away. There are more details on how to delete your account.
