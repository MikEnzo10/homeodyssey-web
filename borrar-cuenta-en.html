How to delete your HomeOdyssey account

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open HomeOdyssey and sign in with your adult account.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Without the app

Email us from your account’s email address at privacy@homeodyssey.app with the subject “Delete my HomeOdyssey account”. We will delete it within 30 days at most and confirm it to you.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email, name and password.
  • The households you created, with all their adventures, missions, rewards and alerts.
  • The profiles of the children in those households and all their progress in the game.
  • The devices registered to receive notifications.

If you were only a member of someone else’s household, your membership is deleted and that household carries on for everyone else. Technical backups disappear on their own within a few days.

More information in the privacy policy.
